# Code of Conduct

Lookshift follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, or sexual or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Don't share anyone's private information, including images of their face, without their permission.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/samapara). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions, or block people, who don't follow this code.
